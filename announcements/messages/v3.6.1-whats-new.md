# Welcome to v3.6.1!

I teamed up with the creator of the **Punchy** mod to bring you first-person petting, lifting, and yeet-charging animations.

**Note:** You must have the Punchy mod (v2.6.0+) installed to see the new hands-on animations. Otherwise, you'll just have to use your imagination.

### Main Highlights
*   **The Yeet Queue:** You now have to hold your throw key for a split second to charge the yeet. Comes with an eager butt-wiggle animation.
*   **Rodent Games:** Hamsters now spontaneously play Hide & Seek inside blocks, and they play Tag with each other.
*   **Agricultural Vandalism:** Wandering hamsters will now casually harvest your mature crops, stuff their cheeks, and make a mess. You can turn this off in the config if you hate fun.
*   **Aggression States:** Feed them specific items to turn them into total pacifists, or unleash them as menacing hunters.
*   **Glowing Armor Trims:** Hamster armor now supports vanilla trims. And yes, they natively glow in the dark (configurable).
*   **New Music Discs:** 3 new custom music discs. They are fully licensed and hamsters will dance to them!

I also added a ton of new configurability, fixed their swimming physics, and added storage crates, and more!
