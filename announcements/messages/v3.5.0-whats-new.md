# Welcome to v3.5.0!

You made the right choice. Here is how to utilize your new, slightly more dangerous rodents.

### How to Tree Heist
1.  Find an **Oak Tree**.
2.  **Yeet** your hamster at the leaves (or dismount while looking at them).
3.  Wait. Your hamster will rummage through the canopy and drop **Acorns**.
4.  *Note: Trees run out of loot eventually. Don't be greedy.*

### Armor & Crafting
*   **Acorn Armor:** Process Acorns in a **Stonecutter** to get shards. Combine with the **Acorn Hat** to craft the base armor. It negates 100% of damage until it breaks.
*   **Upgrades:** Find **Hamster Smithing Templates** in loot chests to upgrade to Iron, Gold, Diamond, or Netherite.
*   **Perks:** Iron makes them more aerodynamic. Gold makes them fast. Netherite makes them heavy. Diamond stops them from stealing your diamonds.

### Bed Respawning
If you are tired of your hamsters dying to lava, check the **Config**. You can now enable **"Respawn in Bed"**. If linked to a Hamster Bed, they will rematerialize there instead of entering the void.
