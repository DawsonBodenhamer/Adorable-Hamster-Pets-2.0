# Welcome to v3.6.0!

You made the right choice. Welcome to the era of procedural genetics.

### Breeding & Genetics
1.  **New Variants:** 18 new community textures plus a new procedurally generated texture system. The result? Over 3000 new wild hamster types.
2.  **The Genetic Blender:** Breed them to mix their DNA. There are over 2.2 million combinations, including hidden recessive red eyes.
3.  **Visualization Tools:** Wondering how it all works? Head over **The Kitchen Drawer** category and look for the new **Admin Commands** chapter.

### New Mechanics
*   **Play Tag:** Gaze into a hamster's eyes until it gets rambunctious and runs away. Catch it before it gets bored, and it will spit a gift out of its cheeks for you.
*   **GUI Renaming:** Open their inventory and click the pencil icon to rename them.
*   **Safety Nets:** Teleport rescue, owner void death rescue, and owner friendly-fire settings.

### Massive FPS Boost
The rendering engine was rewritten so you can have way more hamsters on screen. If your PC still begs for mercy, consider touching grass.
